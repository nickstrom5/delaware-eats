Where to eat scrapple in Delaware

7 places serving scrapple, Delaware's breakfast meat, from a Smyrna sausage house to Wilmington brunch menus, each checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source.

Pork, cornmeal and a hot griddle

Scrapple is pork scraps and trimmings cooked down with cornmeal and spices, set in a loaf, sliced thin and fried crisp. Sussex County is home to RAPA Scrapple in Bridgeville, and Bridgeville's Apple-Scrapple Festival celebrates it every October. Breakfast is its natural home, but brunch kitchens now put it in cheesesteaks, Benedicts and bao buns.

Towns with the most: Wilmington (2), Newark (2), Odessa (1), Middletown (1), Smyrna (1).
