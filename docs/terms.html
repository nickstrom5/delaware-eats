Terms of use

The plain-language terms for the Delaware Eats app and this website. Last updated 2026-10-27.

What the app is

Delaware Eats is a free guide to restaurants in Delaware. It is provided as is, for personal use, without charge and without warranties of any kind.

Check before you go

Restaurants open, close, change their hours and change their menus, and many beach-town places and creameries close for the winter or keep short hours. Dishes and founding years are what each place or a named source said when we checked in fall 2026. We work to keep the lists right, but we can't promise that any listing is current or complete. Call the restaurant before you make the trip.

Inspection results and public records

The app and this site show the Delaware Division of Public Health's inspection records as it publishes them: each inspection's date and type and the Food Code sections cited, with the number cited at a place's latest routine inspection. Delaware has no restaurant grade, score or placard, and we compute none. An inspection is a snapshot of one day. Business licenses come from the Delaware Division of Revenue; a license shows a business is licensed, not that it's open. The agencies provide their data as is and make no warranty of its accuracy or completeness. For official records, ask the agency itself: DPH's Office of Food Protection is at 302-744-4546.
